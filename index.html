What is OSINT?

OSINT is a process, not a magic button.

01 / THINK

Start with a question

Define exactly what you need to know. Separate facts you already have from assumptions and unknowns.

02 / SEARCH

Collect public clues

Use search engines, images, maps, archives, public records and community sources to build a trail.

03 / VERIFY

Corroborate

Compare independent sources, dates and context. A single match is usually a clue—not proof.

The OSINT loop

Repeat the loop until the evidence is strong enough—or you reach its limits.

Tools from the OSINT video

The tools below were taken from the video list you provided. IKnowWhatYouDownload is intentionally excluded.

PimEyes ↗Face & image search. Searches publicly accessible images using visual similarity and face-focused matching. Useful for checking where an image appears online. Use only with appropriate consent and legal grounds.
Osintgram ↗Instagram OSINT tool. An interactive command-line project for collecting publicly available information associated with Instagram accounts. Availability and supported features can change as Instagram changes its platform.
Epieos ↗Email & phone OSINT. Provides search and investigation services designed to help collect and analyze open-source information from identifiers such as email addresses and phone numbers.
IntelTechniques ↗OSINT search toolbox. A large collection of search helpers covering names, usernames, email addresses, phone numbers, domains, IPs, social networks and other public sources. Queries are generated and executed in your browser.
Social Searcher ↗Public social-media search. Helps discover publicly indexed mentions, profiles, posts, images and hashtags. It does not access private social-media content.
Getcontact ↗Caller ID & spam protection. Uses community information to identify callers and flag spam. Privacy note: its policy says some features can involve sharing names, numbers and contact-list information when permissions are enabled.
FreeCarrierLookup ↗Phone carrier lookup. Identifies the telecommunications carrier and line type for phone numbers in the United States and Canada. It is not a precise location tracker.
PhoneInfoga ↗Phone-number OSINT tool. An open-source utility for gathering publicly available information and metadata related to a phone number. It is software, not a magic way to reveal a person's private location.
ExifTool ↗Metadata analysis. A platform-independent tool for reading, writing and editing metadata in many file formats, including EXIF, GPS, IPTC and XMP.
Maltego ↗Link analysis & investigations. Combines public data sources and visualizes relationships between entities such as people, organizations, domains, IPs and documents.
Have I Been Pwned ↗Breach exposure check. Lets you check whether an email address appears in known data breaches and set up notifications for future exposure. Use it for your own accounts or where you have authorization.
IPinfo ↗IP & network intelligence. Provides data associated with an IP address or ASN, including network operator, approximate geographic information, anonymization indicators and related infrastructure data. IP geolocation is not precise device tracking.

Evidence & ethics

Public does not automatically mean appropriate to publish.
Do not publish passwords, tokens or unnecessary private information. Do not stalk, harass, impersonate or attempt unauthorized access. Keep a source trail, record dates, distinguish observations from interpretations, and report only what the evidence actually supports.
